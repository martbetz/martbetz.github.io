Today we say goodbye to Sir Patrick Stewart, a man whose voice, presence, intelligence, and humanity left an indelible mark on generations around the world. 

Long before international fame, he was a distinguished stage actor of the Royal Shakespeare Company, bringing Shakespeare's greatest characters to life with unmatched skill and dedication. His journey from Yorkshire to the world stage remains one of the great stories of British acting.

Yet beyond the awards, acclaim, and iconic roles, what many people admired most was the man himself. He carried dignity without arrogance, authority without cruelty, and wisdom without pretension. Through his performances and public life, he championed kindness, human rights, and empathy.

For millions of fans, he was a constant companion across decades. His characters inspired us to be better, to think more deeply, and to face uncertainty with courage. The worlds of science fiction, theatre, film, and television are poorer for his passing, but richer for the legacy he leaves behind.

A star has gone out, but its light continues to travel.

Fair winds and following seas, Captain.
