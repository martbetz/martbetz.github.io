Despite it's advancing age, my camera really is in good nick; almost like new, in fact, both cosmetically <i>and</i> functionally. So why not continue to use it? Well, here's the thing; ideally, I'd really like it to remain that way.</p>

The passage of time, though, has steadily been stacking the odds:

1. **health issues** – recent health problems have left me with significant neurological challenges that make standing and walking very difficult&nbsp;— I'm notably prone to tripping and falling, and there's a significant risk of me dropping stuff 

2. **after-market support** – Fujifilm no longer offers a repair service for what is now considered vintage models, third-party solutions are getting scarcer, and parts are getting harder and more expensive to source in general

3. **demand** – used X-T10s seem to be going for a rather pretty penny these days, with many examples fetching in excess of £450 in decent condition

My solution, then? Drum roll, please... My solution is... an X-T1. 😎 

Wait! What?! An X-T1?! Yep. Well, when you look at it logically, it all makes perfect sense:

1. **practicality** – it's far more robust than my X-T10, and weather sealed to boot; as it'll certainly stand up to a bit of punishment, it's ideal as a daily beater

2. **cost** – XT-1s are notably lower on the price scale than X-T10s; an X-T1 complete with all (or most) of the original accessories and in excellent condition can be snagged for around £350 or less

3. **convenience** – I can pair it with my full existing collection of interchagable lenses and, because it uses exactly the same sensor as my X-T10, nothing needs to be tweaked as far as my workflow is concerned

So, I'm off to the second-hand market. I'll let you know how I get on.
