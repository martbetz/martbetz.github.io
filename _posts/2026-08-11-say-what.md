Really?! An Auto Promura lens that's even rarer than the 20mm C.P. Hi-Lux MC?! Things are certainly looking that way&nbsp;— to date, this Auto Promura-Gekkor 24mm 2.5 is still a genuine unicorn!

I was recently on the lookout for additions to [The Promura Project](https://martbetz.github.io/photography/projects/2022/08/21/the-promura-project.html) when I stumbled upon a video called [Why The ... Doesn't This Lens EXIST!?](https://m.youtube.com/watch?v=Boz4Etm0vk8) by [Adventures With Marley](https://m.youtube.com/@advntrswithmarley).


What excites me most is the apparent excellent performance, very much in keeping with the Auto Promura 28mm and 20mm C.P. Hi-Lux MC lenses&mbsp;— as such, I may well look at adding this lens to the repo.

For now, though, here's all the known info:

- Source: youtube.com

  - Lens: Auto Promura-Gekkor 24mm 2.5 
  - Serial Number: 240077
  - Mount: Praktica (M42)

  - Date Sold: unknown
  - Location: AUS
  - Listed Price: 50 AUS
  - Sale Price: 50 AUS
    
  - Observations: video of faceplate included (filter thread described as "62mm"); video of mount not included (mount described as "M42"); condition described as having "splotches" on front element and "nicks" on the lens hood.

I've not yet done any research myself, but it seems this lens is a genuine mystery. Oh, well... time to roll up my sleeves and dig in!
