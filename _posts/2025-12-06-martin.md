<p>
It’s with heartfelt grief and sorrow that I learn of Martin’s passing. Nothing I could say would ever be of justice&nbsp;— we really have lost one of the very best; an absolute idol and visionary of the modern photographic era.
</p>

<p>
  ​Martin was an undeniable master of visual sociology, capturing the brilliant quirks and subtle beauty of everyday human life&nbsp;— from the crowded beaches of the British summer to the quiet eccentricities of local communities, he held up a mirror to our world with a unique blend of humor and profound honesty.
</p>

<p>
  ​Martin didn’t just take photographs; he taught us how to really look at the world around us, and his work will no doubt continue to inspire countless generations to come. ​Beyond his iconic body of work with Magnum Photos, Martin’s immense generosity also lives on through the legacy of the Martin Parr Foundation. 
</p>
